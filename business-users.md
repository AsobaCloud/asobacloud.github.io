# Business Users

Solutions designed for solar asset owners, O&M teams, and energy managers who need to optimize performance and maximize returns.

---

## Asset Performance Monitoring

### Real-Time Dashboard
Monitor your solar assets in real-time with comprehensive dashboards that provide:

- **Performance Metrics**: Track energy production, efficiency, and capacity factors
- **Alerts & Notifications**: Get instant alerts for performance issues or anomalies
- **Historical Analysis**: Compare current performance with historical data
- **ROI Tracking**: Monitor financial returns and investment performance

### Key Benefits
- **Reduce Downtime**: Identify and address issues before they impact production
- **Optimize Performance**: Use data-driven insights to improve efficiency
- **Maximize Returns**: Ensure your assets are performing at peak capacity
- **Compliance Reporting**: Generate reports for regulatory requirements

## Predictive Maintenance

### AI-Powered Insights
Our predictive maintenance system uses machine learning to:

- **Predict Failures**: Identify potential equipment failures before they occur
- **Optimize Maintenance Schedules**: Reduce unnecessary maintenance while preventing failures
- **Cost Optimization**: Minimize maintenance costs while maximizing uptime
- **Resource Planning**: Plan maintenance activities efficiently

### Maintenance Features
- **Equipment Health Monitoring**: Track the health of inverters, panels, and other equipment
- **Maintenance Recommendations**: Receive AI-generated maintenance recommendations
- **Work Order Management**: Streamline maintenance workflows
- **Vendor Coordination**: Coordinate with maintenance vendors efficiently
