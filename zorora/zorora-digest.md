# Digest

Digest lets you stage articles and market datasets, then synthesize them into a structured energy market and policy digest — a single shareable document rather than a running feed.

## Staging

Articles are added to a digest from Global View's Articles tab, from Deep Research report sources, or directly within Digest itself. Market datasets (commodity, FX, and energy series) can be staged the same way from Global View's Indicators tab.

Article retrieval within Digest is paginated, so large staged sets remain browsable rather than loading as a single long list.

## Synthesis

Once a set of articles and datasets is staged, Digest synthesizes them into a structured output — organized by theme or market rather than in the order items were staged — suitable for circulating as an investment-committee brief or internal market update.
