# Alerts

Alerts monitor topics and sources for new developments, so relevant regulatory, market, or news changes surface without a manual research query.

## Alert Rules

An alert rule defines what to watch: a topic (e.g. a specific jurisdiction's tariff filings), a source (e.g. a specific regulatory feed), or a combination of both. Rules are configurable per user and run independently of any single research session.

## Execution History

Every alert rule keeps an execution history — when it last ran, what it checked, and whether it produced a new match. This makes it possible to confirm an alert is actually running as expected, rather than trusting silence to mean "nothing changed."
