# Asset Management Interface

The Ona Asset Management interface transforms how energy asset owners monitor, maintain, and optimize their distributed energy portfolios. Built on the OODA (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act) intelligence framework, this unified dashboard provides real-time visibility into asset performance, automated anomaly detection, predictive maintenance planning, and financial impact analysis—all in one place.

**Business Value:**
- **Reduce Operational Costs**: Automated monitoring eliminates manual data collection and analysis, reducing labor costs by up to 60%
- **Maximize Revenue**: Early fault detection prevents production losses, protecting $180K-$320K annually per 10MW portfolio
- **Optimize Maintenance Spend**: AI-driven scheduling ensures maintenance happens when it's most cost-effective, reducing unnecessary service calls by 25-40%
- **Improve Decision Speed**: Real-time insights enable faster response times, reducing fault-to-repair cycles from days to hours

## The Complete OODA Workflow

The Asset Management interface implements the complete OODA (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act) intelligence loop:

1. **Observe** (< 5 minutes): Real-time data collection from all assets across your portfolio
2. **Orient** (< 10 minutes): AI analysis identifies anomalies and diagnoses root causes
3. **Decide** (< 15 minutes): Financial impact analysis and maintenance plan generation
4. **Act** (Continuous): Automated work order creation and crew dispatch

This automated workflow operates continuously, 24/7, ensuring your assets are always protected and optimized without requiring constant manual oversight.

**Business Impact:**
- **Faster Response Times**: Issues are detected and addressed in minutes, not days
- **Reduced Labor Costs**: Automation eliminates the need for dedicated monitoring staff
- **Improved Decision Quality**: Data-driven recommendations outperform manual judgment
- **Scalable Operations**: Manage growing portfolios without proportional increases in operational staff
