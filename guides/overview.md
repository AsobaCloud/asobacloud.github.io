# Overview of Guides

This section provides practical, task-oriented guides to help you solve specific problems and achieve your business goals using the Ona Intelligence Layer. These guides are designed for both **Beginners** and **Developers**.

## What You Can Find Here

*   **[Forecasting](./forecasting.md)**: Learn how to generate and interpret forecasts, understand accuracy metrics, and improve the performance of your predictions.
*   **[Data Management](./data-management.md)**: A guide to preparing your data for our platform, including required formats, standardization, and best practices for uploading.
*   **[Portfolio Management](./portfolio-management.md)**: For users managing multiple assets, this guide covers how to analyze portfolio-wide performance, identify outliers, and streamline reporting.
*   **[Developer Guide](./developer-guide.md)**: Information for developers contributing to or building on top of the platform, including coding guidelines and development processes.
