# Guide: Portfolio Management

The Ona Intelligence Layer provides powerful tools for managing and analyzing a portfolio of renewable energy assets. This guide introduces the key concepts of portfolio management on our platform.

*Note: The features described in this guide are part of our enterprise plans and are not available via the freemium API.*

## The Challenge of Portfolio Management

Managing a diverse portfolio of solar assets presents several challenges:

*   **Fragmented Data**: Data is often spread across multiple systems, in different formats, from different manufacturers.
*   **Lack of Standardization**: Comparing the performance of assets from different OEMs is difficult and time-consuming.
*   **Delayed Insights**: It can take days or even weeks to identify underperforming assets, leading to lost revenue.

## Our Solution: A Unified Intelligence Layer

The Ona Intelligence Layer solves these problems by providing a single, unified platform for your entire portfolio.

### 1. Unified Data Governance

Our platform ingests data from any source and any manufacturer, standardizing it into a single, consistent format. This allows you to compare apples to apples across your entire portfolio, regardless of the underlying hardware.

### 2. Cross-Portfolio Learning

One of the most powerful features of our platform is cross-portfolio learning. Our machine learning models are not trained in isolation. Instead, they learn from the data across your entire portfolio.

*   **Faster Deployment**: This means we can achieve high forecast accuracy for a new site in as little as 6 months, compared to the industry standard of 24-40 months.
*   **Improved Accuracy**: By learning from a more diverse dataset, our models can better identify trends and anomalies, leading to more accurate forecasts and earlier fault detection.

### 3. Portfolio-Level Optimization

Instead of optimizing assets on a site-by-site basis, our platform allows you to optimize your entire portfolio.

*   **Identify Underperforming Assets**: Quickly identify assets that are not performing as expected and drill down to diagnose the root cause.
*   **Optimize Maintenance Schedules**: Plan maintenance activities across your portfolio to minimize downtime and maximize revenue.
*   **Lender-Grade Reporting**: Generate lender-grade DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) reports and other financial statements for your entire portfolio with the click of a button.
