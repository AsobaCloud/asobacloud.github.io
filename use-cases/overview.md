# Use Cases Overview

This section showcases real-world use cases and case studies demonstrating how the Ona Intelligence Layer is used to solve complex problems and deliver measurable business value. These stories are designed for **Decision Makers** and **Beginners** who want to understand the practical applications of our technology and see concrete examples of ROI and operational improvements.

Each use case demonstrates different aspects of our platform, from achieving high forecast accuracy with limited data to maintaining operations during data outages, and unifying intelligence across multiple stakeholders for portfolio-wide optimization.

## Quick Start

To understand how our platform delivers value, start with the [Sibaya Casino](./sibaya-casino.md) case study, which demonstrates achieving high forecast accuracy with limited historical data. This example shows how our generic models can provide immediate value even for new installations.

## Use Case Categories

Our use cases demonstrate value across several key areas:

### Forecast Accuracy

Learn how different organizations achieve high forecast accuracy using our platform, even with challenging data conditions. These examples show how proper data preparation and model selection lead to superior results.

### Operational Resilience

See how our platform maintains operations during data outages, equipment failures, and other disruptions. These case studies demonstrate the value of redundant data sources and robust error handling.

### Portfolio Optimization

Discover how organizations scale operations across hundreds of sites, identifying outliers, optimizing maintenance schedules, and streamlining reporting. These examples show the power of portfolio-wide analytics.

Understand the ROI and business impact of implementing our platform, including reduced operational costs, improved maintenance planning, and better energy trading decisions.
