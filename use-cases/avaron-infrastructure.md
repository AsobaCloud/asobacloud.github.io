# Use Case: Avaron Infrastructure

**Unified Intelligence for Multi-Stakeholder Coordination**

This use case demonstrates how the Ona Intelligence Layer can serve as a single source of truth for complex projects involving multiple stakeholders with different priorities.

*   **Industry**: IPP / Asset Management
*   **Stakeholders**: Asset Owner, O&M Contractor, Insurer
*   **Sites**: Multiple

### The Challenge

The Avaron Infrastructure portfolio involved coordinating between the asset owner, the Operations & Maintenance (O&M) contractor, and the project's insurer. Each stakeholder had their own systems and their own view of the data, leading to:

*   **Fragmented Systems**: Data was siloed, requiring manual reconciliation and creating delays.
*   **Conflicting Priorities**: The O&M contractor might want to schedule maintenance at a time that was suboptimal for the asset owner's revenue goals, while the insurer had separate risk considerations.
*   **Lack of a Single Truth**: Disagreements over data and performance were common, leading to inefficient decision-making.

### The Solution: A Unified Data & Economic Layer

The Ona Intelligence Layer was deployed as a unified data layer, ingesting data from all sources and providing a single, consistent view to all stakeholders.

1.  **Unified Data Governance**: By eliminating data silos, the platform ensured that all stakeholders were working from the same information, eliminating reconciliation and data conflicts.
2.  **Economic Optimization**: The platform's economic modeling capabilities were used to align the interests of all stakeholders. For example, the system could analyze the insurance impact of scheduling maintenance at a certain time, allowing the asset owner to make a decision that balanced revenue, operational needs, and risk.
3.  **Customized Intelligence**: While the underlying data was the same for everyone, the platform provided each stakeholder with the specific intelligence they needed, in the format they required.

### The Results

The Ona Intelligence Layer transformed a complex, multi-stakeholder project into a streamlined and efficient operation.

*   **70% Reduction in Penalties**: By providing advance warning of potential grid code violations, the platform led to a 70% reduction in unplanned penalties.
*   **100% Data Reconciliation**: The single source of truth eliminated all data conflicts and the need for manual reconciliation.
*   **Aligned Incentives**: For the first time, the asset owner, O&M contractor, and insurer were able to make decisions based on a shared understanding of the economic and operational realities of the portfolio.
