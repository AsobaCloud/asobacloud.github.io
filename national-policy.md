# National Policy

## Predictive Energy Management Requires National, Structural Coordination

South Africa needs a unified solution: distributed intelligence infrastructure that creates jobs, secures data sovereignty, and ensures energy reliability simultaneously.

### Workforce Paradox

Over 30% youth unemployment persists despite rising technical literacy.

### Grid Vulnerability

Over-centralized system vulnerable to both cyber threats and mechanical disruption.

### Data Extraction

Foreign cloud monopolies maintain extractive data asymmetry. Local AI dependent on external compute infrastructure.
